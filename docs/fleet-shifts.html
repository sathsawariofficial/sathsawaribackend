01 / the modelTwo things, never one

Pick & Drop is the service. A shift is one recurring trip of that service. They live in separate packages, routes and tables.

A registered driver enables Pick & Drop and becomes its only owner. There are no managers or sub-managers, and the admin does not run it. Other drivers ask to join and may propose their vehicles, passengers ask to join, and the owner approves or rejects each request. A driver belongs to at most one service, as owner or member, and so does a passenger.

A driver joins in one of two ways. As a driver, they can be put behind the wheel of the service's shifts and may bring vehicles. With vehicles only, they belong to the service through the vehicles they offer, at least one, and never drive its shifts: the owner puts each of their vehicles on shifts with the owner or another driver behind the wheel, different vehicles on different shifts, even at the same time. They see those shifts, are told what happens to their vehicles, and keep at least one vehicle for as long as they stay.

The owner builds shifts out of what the service has: themselves or an approved driver, an approved vehicle with a seat count, and approved passengers. Every run of a shift on a date is written down as an occurrence, which is what attendance, reminders and travel history hang off.

02 / anatomyWhat a shift actually is

One recurring entity: a driver, a vehicle, a route with times, passengers pinned to its stops, and the days it runs.

A shift is named after its vehicle's number, and renaming the vehicle renames its active shifts and their upcoming trips. It runs on its days of the week (1 is Monday, 7 is Sunday), from its start date until its end date, or until it is changed when there is none. Its route is 2 to 20 places, each reached after the one before, so a trip lasts from the time of the first place to the time of the last and never spans midnight. Each passenger waits at one stop.

Seats are kept on the shift: the total is the vehicle's seat count, the occupied count is kept in step with the passengers on it inside the same transaction, and a database check stops occupied from ever exceeding total. Changing the vehicle's seats changes every active shift on it, and is refused below what a shift already carries.

03 / clashesNobody is in two places at once

A driver, a vehicle or a passenger is never on two shifts whose trips overlap, in any service, a driver or vehicle is never on a carpool ride during a trip, and a driver is never on two carpool rides at once, whichever vehicles they drive.

The test is a real interval overlap, start < other end AND end > other start, so a trip ending at 08:00 leaves everyone free for one starting at 08:00. Times are only half of it: two shifts clash only if there is a date both actually run on. Sharing a weekday is not enough, a Monday to Friday shift that ends on the 10th never meets a Wednesday shift that starts on the 20th, so the check walks the first seven days of the overlap of their date ranges to find a real shared date. Only what is still to come counts, a clash on a date already behind us never stops a shift that has run for months from being edited.

The check reads what is already committed, so every shift create, edit and passenger change, and every ride created or switched back on, takes a transaction scoped advisory lock on each driver, vehicle and passenger involved, in sorted order, before it looks. Rides and shifts share the same lock keys, so an owner putting a driver on a shift and that driver creating a ride at the same moment are serialised instead of both passing.

Removed from this branch

Groups, sub-managers, the role and permission tables and their admin APIs, gender locked seats, shift templates, pickup and drop directions, and the admin screens that managed a group or a shift are gone. Their tables are dropped once at boot when the old schema is detected. Main never had them.

Added back, read only

An admin console still needs to see the whole platform. /api/v1/admin/pickdrop/services, /pickdrop/service, /pickdrop/advertisements, /shifts and /shift/requests let an admin browse and search every service, shift, advertisement and open rider request on the platform, and the overview counts them. None of it can be changed from there — creating, editing or deciding anything about a service or a shift is still only ever the owner's, exactly as above. The overview, service and shift queries are written again in services/admin rather than imported from services/pickdrop or services/shift, because no service package imports another.

06 / tripsTrips, attendance and history

A shift is one row, but every date it runs becomes an occurrence with its own attendance, written ahead by the scheduler.

On every tick the worker writes the trips of the day before, today and the day after, in one statement for every shift, and never for a time before the shift existed. Marking absent days ahead writes that trip on the spot. Each occurrence freezes the driver, vehicle, names and route of that day, which is why travel history still reads true after the shift is edited or deleted, the passenger leaves, or the account is removed.

Attendance is one row per trip and passenger, present unless they mark themselves absent. Who was on a trip is worked out from when each passenger was added to and removed from the shift, so a replacement passenger never inherits a trip made before them, and a removed passenger's row is kept rather than reused.

08 / recordsNothing that mattered is thrown away

Deletions follow the ride share pattern: copy to a del_* table, then hard delete.

  • Shifts go to del_shifts and del_shift_locations. Their shift_passengers, occurrences and attendance stay, which is what shift history, passenger history and travel history read.
  • Advertisements go to del_pick_drop_advertisements and their locations, when deleted or when the service is disabled.
  • Shift requests go to del_shift_requests and their locations.
  • Availability goes to del_passenger_availabilities and its locations when the passenger's account is deleted.
  • Memberships in pick_drop_drivers, pick_drop_vehicles and pick_drop_passengers are never deleted, they end with a status and who ended them when.
  • Deleting a driver or passenger account ends their memberships and takes a passenger off active shifts first, so nothing is left pointing at somebody who no longer exists. ./sath db dangling checks it.
